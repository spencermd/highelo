HighEloReplays is a desktop app used by the owner of the YouTube channel Today on the North American Server to upload that channel's videos. This policy explains what it does with Google account data.

Google data the app accesses

The app asks for one permission: upload videos to your YouTube channel (the youtube.upload scope of the YouTube Data API). It uses it only to upload the channel's own videos, Shorts and thumbnails, with their titles, descriptions and tags. It does not read your email, contacts, files, viewing history, comments or any other account data.

How it is stored

After you sign in, Google gives the app a sign-in token. The token is saved in a file on your own computer and nowhere else. The app has no servers, so nothing about your account is sent to the developer or to anyone other than Google.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising or to train any model. HighEloReplays' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

HighEloReplays uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and data

You can remove the app's access at any time from your Google account's third-party connections page. Deleting the token file on your computer also removes everything the app stored.
