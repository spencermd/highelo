HighEloReplays is a desktop app that makes the daily videos for the YouTube channel Today on the North American Server: the best plays from the North American League of Legends ladder (Challenger, Grandmaster and Master).

What it does

  • Finds the day's best plays from public League of Legends match data.
  • Records them from the game's replays and edits them into a video, a Short and a thumbnail.
  • Uploads those videos to the channel's own YouTube account, using the YouTube Data API.

Who uses it

Only the channel's owner, on their own PC, to upload to their own channel. It is not offered to anyone else and has no accounts, website sign-in or servers.

How the app handles Google account data is described in the privacy policy.
